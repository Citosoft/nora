### Features

- The compact mobile composer adds photo and file attachments with previews and upload status, provider logos, and one menu for permissions and reasoning.
- Choose the model, permissions and reasoning effort in the mobile composer when starting a thread or sending the next message in an existing thread.
- Settle threads in bulk from a compact dialog with a scrolling thread list: preselect by last activity, adjust individual selections, and settle older threads together while keeping their history available to reopen.
- Clicking Nora in the Bots sidebar opens a chat with Nora, like messaging any other bot. Nora hands each request to an ordinary Nora thread, which stays listed under Nora. Choose the model those threads use under Settings → Bots → Nora's chat model.
- Star models in the model picker to add them to a Favourites tab at the top of the provider list, which shows your favourite models from every provider in one place and opens automatically when the current model is a favourite.
- Explore Nora without an account or computer in an offline demo with sample chats, bots, approvals, questions and editable settings.
- The phone app’s About screen shows the installed version, build, running OTA update ID, publish time and update-check status, with a manual check for updates.
- The phone app prompts when an over-the-air update is available, shows download progress, and lets you restart into the new version.
- The mobile app can edit a connected computer’s thread preferences and create or edit its bots, including their instructions and avatars.
- New mobile builds support over-the-air JavaScript and asset updates through EAS Update, reducing the need for TestFlight rebuilds.
- Pin any finished message in a thread from its hover toolbar; pinned messages are listed in a new Pinned messages section of the thread details panel, where clicking one scrolls to it (loading earlier history if needed) and each can be unpinned.
- Approval prompts can switch the thread to automatic review: Claude in Ask permissions or Accept edits mode offers Allow and switch to Auto, which applies straight away, and Codex offers Allow and switch to auto-review, a new Workspace edits · auto-review permission where a Codex reviewer agent answers requests from the next turn.
- Choose which claude.ai connectors a bot can use under Account connectors in the bot editor; bots get only the ones you pick, applied from their next turn, and thread details list only the connectors a bot thread can reach.
- The Nora phone app opens with a short welcome over the website's drifting pixel terrain (chat, approvals and how your computer does the work), with Back, Skip and Continue, ending on sign-in (on the web app, creating an account happens in the same styled panel instead of Clerk's hosted page); once seen, it opens straight on sign-in and the intro is a swipe away.
- The Nora phone app now lands on a Home dashboard: a greeting with what needs you and what's running across all your computers, shortcuts for a new chat, Inbox, Activity and Bots on your main computer, and a card for each computer with its status, live counts and latest chats. Computers moved to their own page, and Settings → Open at launch can still jump straight into one computer.
- Dictate messages with a new microphone button in the thread and bot chat composers: click it to record (live input levels show while listening), click again to transcribe into the message, or press Esc to discard. It uses the provider chosen in Settings → Voice and explains what to set up when dictation isn't configured.

### Improvements

- Nora Relay automatically cleans up disconnected orphan tunnels after 7 days and registered computers after 90 days offline, restoring expired connections when those computers return.
- Mobile threads group tool calls into one updating row per turn, hide blank thinking, and show activity-aware thinking orbs with varied waiting phrases between steps.
- Settings sections are grouped under App, Agents & AI, Connections & tools, and Advanced to make the sidebar easier to scan.
- Thread lists initially show the first 10 threads per group, or 10 total without grouping, with Show more revealing the rest across all thread sources.
- Thread orbs follow provider activity while thinking, searching, calling integrations, running tools and writing replies, with a working fallback; the simulator previews these transitions and all nine styles at three sizes.
- While a bot works on a chat reply, the typing line shows an animated thinking orb (from the open-source thinking-orbs library) that follows light and dark mode and stays still with reduced motion.
- Settings → Voice now offers a Local model dictation engine with a picker of free offline models, replacing the Whisper-only option: Whisper Base and Tiny, plus NVIDIA Parakeet v2 (English) and v3 (25 European languages), which install their sherpa-onnx runtime automatically. Each model shows its maker's logo (OpenAI or NVIDIA), and your existing Whisper choice carries over.
- Settings → Appearance → UI Font has an SF Pro option, which uses Apple's San Francisco typeface on macOS (and installed SF Pro fonts elsewhere).
- Nora's logo inside the app (sidebar, chats and the start-up screen) now uses the borderless mark from the App Store icon instead of the version with the green outline.
- The Nora Relay settings page is redesigned. This computer's name is at the top, with Rename and Reset connection in a ⋯ menu. Settings are grouped into cards with compact switches. A small QR code installs the iPhone beta from TestFlight, and the web-app link has Copy and Open buttons. Recent activity folds away in its own card, and the section has a phone icon in the sidebar. A small phone-to-computer animation beside the Nora Relay description shows packets moving while the relay is connected.
- Remote access is now called Nora Relay across Settings, the Mobile page, the tray menu and the phone app.
- The full and single-file diff viewers match the rest of the app: each file is a collapsible card with a status badge and line counts, and lines are larger with old/new line numbers and syntax highlighting, with no raw git header lines.
- Large diffs open faster and use far less memory: the full diff viewer only draws files near the visible area, and long files show 500 lines at a time with Show more and Show all buttons.
- Files in a turn's Edited files card whose patch is too large to preview have an Open in diff viewer link that opens the full diff scrolled to that file, or says the file has no uncommitted changes left.
- Permissions and reasoning have their own pickers beside the model picker, each listing its options with an icon and a plain description of what it does; Full access is highlighted as a warning, and Claude and Codex permission modes have clearer names such as Ask permissions, Full access and Workspace edits.
- Suggested replies can be turned off in Settings → Threads → Claude to save tokens, and Claude bot threads and chats no longer generate them.
- Memory reviews and other background Claude tasks run on Haiku unless you choose a model, instead of your plan's default model such as Opus.
- App builder threads stop re-reading the builder guide and the app before every change, and a change made against an outdated version of the app is refused instead of overwriting it.
- Claude bot threads, chats and app builders no longer load all your claude.ai connectors and synced plugins and skills, cutting tens of thousands of tokens from every turn; your own work threads keep them.
- Settings → Threads is split into tabs: All providers for the settings every provider uses, then a tab per provider for the ones only it supports; Claude's tab holds the subagent model, suggested replies, compaction threshold and idle chat compaction, and settings search opens the right tab.
- Sidebar thread popovers show the thread's model and use a bold title.
- Hovering the account avatar while signed out shows a small animated card explaining that signing in unlocks remote control from your phone with Nora Relay and settings sync across devices; reply packets in the Nora Relay diagrams now use a neutral colour so they stand apart from requests.
- The thread list's refresh button sits in the Threads header beside the view options, instead of below the list, and reloads Nora's threads as well as rescanning other tools.
- Unstarted draft threads with no provider yet show a draft icon where the provider logo sits, so they line up with the rest of the thread list.
- The sidebar footer has fewer icons, now grouped together on the right instead of spread across the width: Keyboard shortcuts moved into the Help menu, and Connections and Model usage moved to the bottom of the left icon strip above Mobile.
- Back-to-back agent messages within a turn now sit close together; their copy and pin toolbar floats over the message on hover instead of leaving a gap below it.
- Desktop text is one pixel smaller throughout the app, including menus, chats, editors and terminals.
- Mobile bot creation and editing are available directly from the sidebar and bot chats, with computer settings also in the sidebar and computer menu.
- Inactive tabs in the workspace tab row have a subtle background so you can see where each tab starts and ends.
- Collapsed thread groups in the sidebar sit compactly together.
- The Nora phone app's screens share one monochrome visual language, with black and white buttons, links and selections instead of green: consistent headings, list rows, section headers, avatars and code text, the pixel Nora mark in the sidebar, loading and update screens, and a clearer unread dot in the inbox.
- The Nora phone app's launch screen shows the plain dot N (black on white, or white on black in dark mode) instead of the green-bordered logo, and its Android, web and home-screen icons use the same mono icon as the App Store.

### Fixes

- App launches are counted before analytics opt-in by OS, country and app version using a separate random installation ID; detailed usage stays opt-in, with an explanation in Privacy settings.
- The Nora web app no longer gets stuck on a blank screen when it is opened during a deploy; it now refuses to cache a page in place of its app files and fixes itself on the next visit.
- Settings sections in the sidebar now highlight on hover.
- The iPhone app download button now opens the current Nora TestFlight beta.
- The floating thread details panel can be clicked again on wide windows; the transcript no longer sits on top of it.
- Signing in from the corrected Nora TestFlight app now uses an allowed Clerk callback for `com.citosoft.nora`.
- Nora’s iOS app uses the Citosoft bundle identifier (`com.citosoft.nora`), matching Meta FPL’s naming convention.
- The sidebar thread list no longer reshuffles on its own: Claude and Codex conversations started outside Nora are found once when the list first opens, and again only when you press refresh.
