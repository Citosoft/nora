### Features

- The workspace home shows the branch's PR or MR with its changed files, uncommitted files and commits ahead of the base branch, lists the next tasks from the task board with a button to start a thread on each, and lets you approve or answer waiting requests right there.
- The thread sidebar has a collapsible Settled section at the bottom that opens to show matches while searching, so settled threads can be found by name and reopened without switching filters.
- A Mobile button at the bottom of the left icon strip opens a full-window page showing the Nora phone app in action (threads, bots, approvals, messages and notifications), with a link to the iPhone beta on TestFlight and buttons to sign in and enable remote access. A diagram on the page explains how Nora Connect links your phone to this computer, and once remote access is on the page lists the devices that have signed in, showing which are connected now, with a Disconnect button that cuts off one device until it signs in again.

### Improvements

- When threads are grouped, each group has its own Show more for older threads from other tools, instead of one button at the bottom of the list.
- Thread group headings in the sidebar have a dotted rule beneath them and can be clicked to collapse or expand their group.
- Bots' work threads are now hidden from thread lists by default; show them again from the thread view options.
- The workspace home page is redesigned around what you do next. Type in the box at the top to open a new thread with your words ready to send. Threads are grouped into Needs you, In progress and Pick up where you left off, each with its provider logo or bot avatar. Project commands and tools sit in a column alongside.
- The split-view selector and New view button are gone from the title bar.
- In the Nora phone app's sidebar, workspaces are now chosen from one compact switcher row, like on desktop, instead of a long list of workspaces.
- Chats in the Nora phone app's sidebar show the logo of the agent they run on, such as Claude Code or Codex.

### Fixes

- Enabling Antigravity in Settings no longer opens the Antigravity IDE; Nora now only uses the `agy` CLI.
- The Nora iPhone app no longer crashes after connecting to a computer; times such as "5 minutes ago" now render on iOS.
- In the Nora phone app, the on-screen keyboard no longer covers the message box, so you can see what you're typing in chats, new chats and bot chats.
- The Nora iPhone app now gets live updates from your computer instead of staying on "Reconnecting…", because remote access accepts the iPhone app's connection.
- The Nora iPhone app's icon is now a solid, full-bleed tile, so it shows correctly on the home screen and the App Store instead of with transparent corners.
