## Description

Nora on your phone keeps work moving when you step away from your desk.

Nora Desktop runs your AI coding agents on your own computer. The Nora app links to it securely, so you can check in, steer and unblock your agents from anywhere.

FOLLOW YOUR THREADS
See every thread your agents are working on, with live progress as they edit code, run tests and open pull requests.

ANSWER APPROVALS
When an agent needs a decision or permission, it lands in your inbox. Approve, decline or answer its question in a tap.

SEND INSTRUCTIONS
Start a new chat in any project, reply to a running thread or stop a task, right from your phone.

WORK ACROSS COMPUTERS
Sign in with the same account you use in Nora Desktop and your linked computers appear automatically. Switch between them at any time.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your phone connects directly to your own computer through Nora Connect. Your code and conversations stay on your machine; Nora's servers only list which computers are linked to your account.

Requires Nora Desktop running on a Mac, Windows or Linux computer with remote access turned on. Get it at withnora.run.
